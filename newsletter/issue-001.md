## Fee corrections we caught

- **PSLV:** Sprott lists a **0.52%** MER. etfdb.com showed **0.67%**.
- **PHYS:** Sprott lists a **0.39%** MER. etfdb.com showed **0.41%**.
- **SIVR:** the **0.30%** fee is **net of a voluntary 0.15% waiver**. The gross fee is **0.45%** (SEC 10-Q, quarter ended June 30, 2026), and the sponsor can end the waiver.

When sources disagree, we use the issuer's figure. [How we check →](https://smith83975-sys.github.io/metalfundcompare/methodology/)

## One comparison worth a look: SLV vs PSLV vs SIVR

All three hold silver. Here's what fees would cost on $10,000 over 10 years if the silver price stayed flat:

- SIVR (0.30%): **$296**
- SLV (0.50%): **$489**
- PSLV (0.52%): **$508**

SLV is by far the biggest and most traded: about $830M a day over the last 30 sessions, vs. roughly $122M for PSLV. US tax differs too: SLV and SIVR are taxed as collectibles (28% max long-term rate), while PSLV is a Canadian trust under PFIC rules (QEF election).
