MetalFund Weekly Brief #1 · Oct 16, 2026

Sprott's silver trust is trading 4.94% below its silver

Data as of US close Thursday, October 15, 2026 (site update run 2026-10-16 08:55 UTC)

Hi, and welcome to the first issue. Each week you'll get the numbers behind gold and silver funds, all sourced from issuers and SEC filings. Data below is as of the US close on Thursday, October 15, 2026.

This week in numbers

Sprott trusts vs. NAV (Sprott's published figures, Oct 15):

  • PSLV (silver): −4.94%
  • CEF (gold + silver): −4.45%
  • SPPP (platinum + palladium): −4.12%
  • PHYS (gold): −2.41%

All four closed below the value of the metal they hold. See the discount tracker →

Lowest stated fees:

  • Physical gold: IAUM 0.09%, then GLDM 0.10%
  • Physical silver: SIVR 0.30% (see the waiver note below), then YSAG 0.39%

Fee corrections we caught

  • PSLV: Sprott lists a 0.52% MER. etfdb.com showed 0.67%.
  • PHYS: Sprott lists a 0.39% MER. etfdb.com showed 0.41%.
  • SIVR: the 0.30% fee is net of a voluntary 0.15% waiver. The gross fee is 0.45% (SEC 10-Q, quarter ended June 30, 2026), and the sponsor can end the waiver.

When sources disagree, we use the issuer's figure. How we check →

One comparison worth a look: SLV vs PSLV vs SIVR

All three hold silver. Here's what fees would cost on $10,000 over 10 years if the silver price stayed flat:

  • SIVR (0.30%): $296
  • SLV (0.50%): $489
  • PSLV (0.52%): $508

SLV is by far the biggest and most traded: about $830M a day over the last 30 sessions, vs. roughly $122M for PSLV. US tax differs too: SLV and SIVR are taxed as collectibles (28% max long-term rate), while PSLV is a Canadian trust under PFIC rules (QEF election). Our tax guide explains →
