PPLT (abrdn Physical Platinum Shares ETF) vs SPPP (Sprott Physical Platinum and Palladium Trust)

The short version

  • PPLT has the lowest stated expense ratio (0.60%). On $10,000, that is about $18 a year less than SPPP (0.78%), or about $169 over 10 years if the metal price stayed flat.
  • Structure differs: PPLT is a US grantor trust that holds the metal; SPPP is a Canadian closed-end trust that holds the metal and can trade away from NAV.

  • PPLT trades the most (about $27.7M a day over the last 30 sessions), which usually means tighter spreads.
