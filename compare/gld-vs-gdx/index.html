GLD (SPDR Gold Shares) vs GDX (VanEck Gold Miners ETF)

The short version

  • GLD has the lowest stated expense ratio (0.40%). On $10,000, that is about $11 a year less than GDX (0.51%), or about $106 over 10 years if the metal price stayed flat.
  • Structure differs: GLD is a US grantor trust that holds the metal; GDX is a fund of mining-company stocks.
  • US tax treatment differs: GLD: Collectibles (28% max LT); GDX: Regular fund (1099). See the tax guide.
  • GLD is the largest by assets ($134.4B).
  • GLD trades the most (about $3.1B a day over the last 30 sessions), which usually means tighter spreads.
