Methodology & sources

Verification levels

  • issuer-verified ({{ counts['issuer-verified'] }} funds): fee, assets and inception checked on the issuer's own website or SEC filings.
  • partly verified ({{ counts['partly verified'] }}): fee checked against the issuer or SEC filing; some other fields from third parties.
  • unverified ({{ counts['unverified'] }}): mostly third-party data (e.g. etfdb.com). These numbers are shown with a † and a grey colour.

When sources disagree, the issuer wins. Examples found while building this site: etfdb listed PSLV at 0.67% and PHYS at 0.41%; Sprott's own pages show MERs of 0.52% and 0.39%. SIVR's sponsor fee is 0.45% gross, reduced to 0.30% by a voluntary waiver the sponsor can end (SEC 10-Q, June 2026).

Data sources

  • Expense ratios: issuer fund pages; SEC 10-K/10-Q/424B3 filings (EDGAR).
  • Assets & NAV: SSGA NAV history files (GLD, GLDM), iShares product data (IAU, IAUM, SLV, RING, SLVP), Sprott trust pages, Aberdeen fund pages, U.S. Global and Y'all Street pages.
  • Prices & volume: Nasdaq historical quotes (daily close).
  • Premium/discount: Sprott's published figure for its trusts; otherwise computed as close ÷ issuer NAV − 1 for the same date. Values beyond ±3% for ETFs are flagged "check" because they usually mean a date mismatch. Computed ETF values are labelled indicative: US metal trusts strike NAV at the metal benchmark earlier in the day, so the 4pm close vs NAV gap mostly reflects metal moves after the benchmark.
