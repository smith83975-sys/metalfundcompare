Fee drag calculator

An expense ratio is taken every year, whatever the metal does. Pick up to three funds (or type your own fee) to see what fees cost you.

Method: ending value = amount × ((1 + growth) × (1 − fee))years; fee cost = value without fees − value with fees. Ignores trading costs, spreads, premiums/discounts and taxes. Fees marked † are third-party figures not yet verified with the issuer.
